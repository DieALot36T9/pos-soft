# Cashier Guide

> This is an outline. The full text, with screenshots, is written in M14 in plain English. Most of it is also inside the app as tooltips.

1. **Logging in and out**: your tile, PIN, locking the till (F12)
2. **Starting your shift**: counting the float
3. **Selling**: scanning, searching, quantities (F4, `3*`), removing lines
4. **Weighed items**: the scale, manual weight, scale stickers
5. **Customers**: finding by phone (F3), adding a new customer, points
6. **Discounts**: when you can, and when an admin must approve
7. **Taking payment**: cash and change, card, mobile wallet, split payments, customer credit, points
8. **Holding and resuming sales** (F5/F6)
9. **Receipts**: reprinting the last receipt (Ctrl+P), when the printer fails
10. **Returns and exchanges**: what you need (admin approval)
11. **Cash in and out of the drawer**
12. **Ending your shift**: X report, counting cash, the Z report
13. **If something goes wrong**: power cut, printer, scanner, scale
14. **Shortcut card** (printable): [[Keyboard Shortcuts]]
